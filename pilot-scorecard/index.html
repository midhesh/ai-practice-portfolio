AI Pilot Scorecard

MIT's 2025 "State of AI in Business" study found roughly 95% of generative AI pilots fail to reach production or deliver measurable ROI — not because the technology fails, but because success criteria are rarely defined before the trial starts, so results get judged by feeling afterward. (source) This tool forces that definition upfront, adds a responsible-use check, then gives an evidence-based verdict instead of a vibe-based one.

Define the pilot

What is the professional providing, what is the AI actually doing, and what does the human still decide? If you can't fill in all three separately, the pilot isn't scoped yet.
02

Success criteria — set before running the pilot

A number and a threshold, not a feeling. If this can't be filled in before the pilot starts, that's the actual problem to fix first.
03

Responsible-use risk check

Checked items don't block the pilot — they raise the bar for what counts as "ready to scale," regardless of how good the metric looks.
